FARMING SOLUTIONS FAQ

Everything You Need To Know About Modern Farming.

Learn more about hydroponic systems, vertical farming, irrigation, lighting, nutrients, monitoring equipment, and solutions for different growing environments.

Modern growing solutions can include hydroponic systems, vertical racks and towers, indoor growing setups, irrigation systems, grow lighting, nutrients, growing media, and farm monitoring equipment.

The right system depends on the crops you want to grow, available space, growing environment, water availability, production goals, and the level of automation required. Different systems are suited to different growing conditions and crop types.

Yes. Vertical racks, towers, and modular growing structures can make use of vertical space instead of relying only on floor area. The appropriate setup depends on ceiling height, access, lighting, irrigation, and the crops being cultivated.

Depending on the growing method, an indoor setup may require growing structures, irrigation equipment, reservoirs, pumps, LED grow lights, airflow equipment, timers, controllers, nutrients, growing media, and environmental monitoring tools.

pH affects nutrient availability in the growing solution, while EC provides an indication of dissolved nutrient concentration. Monitoring these values helps growers manage the nutrient solution according to the crop and growing stage.

Irrigation delivers water and, in many hydroponic systems, nutrient solution to the plants. Pumps, reservoirs, tubing, filters, emitters, and controllers can work together to provide consistent water delivery.

Depending on the system, growers may use materials such as coco coir, rockwool, clay pebbles, perlite, or other suitable soilless growing media. The choice depends on the crop, system design, moisture management, and root-support requirements.

Yes. Commercial growing projects can be designed around the available facility, crop requirements, production capacity, irrigation layout, lighting requirements, monitoring equipment, and level of automation needed.
