FAQ

Questions About Modern Growing?

What is hydroponic farming?

Hydroponic farming is a method of growing plants without traditional soil, using a managed growing environment and nutrient solution.

What equipment is needed to start hydroponic growing?

Requirements depend on the growing method, crop, environment, and system design. Typical components can include a growing system, irrigation, nutrients, growing media, and suitable lighting.

Is vertical farming suitable for small spaces?

Vertical systems can make use of vertical space, making them suitable for a range of compact growing environments.

Can GreenGrow help with commercial systems?

GreenGrow provides product categories and solutions for growers exploring larger and more structured modern farming setups.
