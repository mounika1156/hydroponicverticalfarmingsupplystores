FARM COMPONENTS

The Components Behind Every Growing System.

From water delivery and plant support to lighting and environmental control, every component plays an important role in building an efficient modern growing system.

01

Growing Racks

Multi-level structures designed to growing space in controlled environments infarming system.

02

Hydroponic Channels

Channels that deliver nutrient-rich water directly to plant roots for efficient soil-free cultivation.

03

Reservoirs

Water storage containers that hold and supply the nutrient solution throughout the growing system.

04

Pumps

Circulate water and nutrients between reservoirs, channels, tanks, and irrigation points to modern supply.

05

Irrigation Tubing

Flexible tubing and connectors used to distribute water efficiently across growing areas.

06

Nutrients

Essential mineral solutions that provide plants with the elements required for healthy growth.

07

Growing Media

Root-support materials that provide structure, moisture retention, and airflow around plant roots.

08

LED Grow Lights

Efficient artificial lighting designed to provide plants with controlled light for indoor cultivation.

09

pH & EC Meters

Monitoring tools used to measure solution acidity and nutrient concentration and tubing growth.

10

Timers & Controllers

Automation tools that help manage lighting, irrigation cycles, pumps, and growing schedules.

A modern growing system works as a connected ecosystem, where each component supports water delivery, plant health, lighting, monitoring, or environmental control.
