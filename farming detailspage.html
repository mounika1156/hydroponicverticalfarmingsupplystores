A Smarter Way To Grow.

Hydroponic farming replaces traditional soil cultivation with controlled water, nutrients and growing environments.

GreenGrow provides the essential products and components required to build efficient hydroponic growing systems. From compact home gardens to larger controlled environments, every setup can be configured around your growing requirements.

SYSTEM OPTIONS

Choose The Right Growing System.

Different hydroponic methods can be configured according to available space, crop type and growing goals.

View System Products
01

NFT Systems

Nutrient-rich water flows through growing channels, providing roots with continuous access to water and nutrients.

02

DWC Systems

Plants grow with their roots suspended in nutrient-rich water supported by an aerated growing environment.

03

Drip Systems

Nutrient solution is delivered directly to the growing media through controlled irrigation.

04

Recirculating Systems

Water and nutrients can be circulated through a connected growing system for efficient resource management.

GROWING ENVIRONMENT

Control The Growing Environment.

Successful controlled cultivation depends on balancing several important environmental factors.

Light

Provide suitable light for the growing environment.

Water

Maintain consistent and efficient water delivery.

Nutrients

Supply plants with the nutrients required for growth.

Airflow

Support healthy airflow throughout the growing space.
