01
PRIVACY & TRUST

Introduction

GreenGrow respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, creating an account, requesting information or purchasing our hydroponic and vertical farming products.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we use it, how it may be shared, how we protect it and the choices available to you.

By using the GreenGrow website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will request consent before using information for purposes that require consent.

02
DATA COLLECTION

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with GreenGrow, we may collect different categories of information.

Account Information

Name, email address, phone number and information associated with your GreenGrow customer account.

Order Information

Product selections, billing and delivery details, order history and information needed to process purchases.

Enquiry Information

Information you provide when contacting us about products, farming systems, projects, quotations or support.

Technical Information

Browser, device, approximate location, IP address and website interaction data, where collected by our services.

03
PURPOSE

How We Use Information

We may use information we collect for purposes such as operating our website, providing products and services, processing orders and communicating with customers.

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process purchases, quotations and enquiries.
  • Arrange product delivery and order support.
  • Improve website performance and customer experience.
  • Detect fraud, abuse and security issues.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted and where you have chosen to receive them.
04
COMMERCE

Orders & Payments

When you place an order with GreenGrow, we use the information necessary to process the purchase, arrange fulfilment and provide customer support.

Payment information may be processed through third-party payment providers. GreenGrow should not store complete payment-card information unless its actual payment infrastructure is specifically designed and authorized to do so.

Payment Processing Payments may be handled by secure third-party payment providers according to their own privacy and security practices.
05
WEBSITE TECHNOLOGY

Cookies & Tracking

GreenGrow may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the website functioning, remember preferences, understand website usage and, where permitted, support analytics or marketing.

Essential

Required for core website functionality, security and account features.

Analytics

Helps us understand how visitors use and navigate the website.

Marketing

May be used to measure or personalize marketing where permitted.

Cookie availability and consent requirements can depend on the visitor's location and the services configured on the website.

06

If you choose to receive marketing communications, GreenGrow may send information about products, new farming solutions, promotions, educational content and other relevant updates.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link included in the message or by contacting GreenGrow.

08
PROTECTION

Data Security

GreenGrow takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Protecting Your Information

Access to personal information should be limited to people and service providers who need it for legitimate business purposes.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

GreenGrow retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including fulfilling orders, maintaining accounts, resolving disputes, preventing fraud and meeting applicable legal or accounting requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.
